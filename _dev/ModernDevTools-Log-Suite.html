Modern Dev Tools — suite-standard studies

Five fully interactive restyles of the debug log, bridging the iOS studies onto the shipped suite standard (Modern Notifications · Needs Tab · Health Tab · Colonist Bar). What changed to close the gap: iOS system blue → the suite accent #73BFFF with ink text; true black + vibrancy blur → the suite's blue-tinted opaque neutrals (IMGUI-honest — no backdrop blur anywhere); segmented iOS controls → capsule pills and tab pills with counts; plus the suite's signature atoms — uppercase micro-labels, dim-key/bright-value chips, thin meters, solid amber/red banner alerts, key-value cards, roster rows with accent bars, tabular numerals. Everything clicks: filters, search, selection, switches, pins, ignore/undo, banners, charts, stacks.
